## Handling a blocked integration

Record the missing input, responsible person, affected acceptance IDs and safe disabled state. Complete independent work. Do not invent credentials, guess contact identities, fabricate provider success, or make live calls to customers as a test.

## Defaults

Initial display defaults: English, INR and Asia/Kolkata. These are implementation defaults for this India-based brief, not inferred tax rules. Keep them centralised and confirm real financial settings before live invoice issue. Default advance policy must never bypass the confirmed received-advance gate.

The company's Refrens profile is verified as the brand source. Refrens is not an accounting-integration requirement: the portal maintains its own accounts, and production financial settings still require the firm's actual configuration.
